# Abstract

Artificial Intelligence has become one of the most transformative technologies of the modern era. However, AI projects often combine software, datasets, models, prompts, APIs and autonomous agents under licensing frameworks that were not originally designed for these components.

The **Free AI License (FAIL)** was created to provide an open licensing framework specifically designed for Artificial Intelligence while remaining simple, transparent and practical for developers, organizations, researchers and businesses.

This whitepaper introduces the philosophy, objectives, architecture and governance of the FAIL ecosystem.

---

# Executive Summary

FAIL is more than a software license.

It is an open ecosystem composed of:

- An AI-focused license
- Technical specifications
- Documentation standards
- Machine-readable metadata
- Compliance guidelines
- Public project registry
- Certification framework
- Governance processes

Together these components establish a common foundation for developing, distributing and maintaining Artificial Intelligence projects.

---

# The Problem

Traditional software licenses were written long before the emergence of modern AI systems.

Artificial Intelligence projects frequently include:

- Large Language Models
- Computer Vision Models
- Audio Models
- Datasets
- Synthetic Data
- Prompt Libraries
- AI Agents
- APIs
- Evaluation Benchmarks
- Safety Policies
- Fine-tuned Models

Managing these assets consistently presents legal and technical challenges that conventional software licenses alone do not fully address.

FAIL seeks to provide a structured framework tailored to AI projects.

# Core Principles

## Freedom to Use

Anyone may use FAIL-licensed projects for lawful purposes.

---

## Freedom to Learn

Developers and researchers should be able to study how AI systems work.

---

## Freedom to Modify

Users may improve, adapt and extend FAIL-licensed projects in accordance with the license.

---

## Freedom to Build

FAIL supports the creation of new applications, services and research built upon licensed projects.

---

## Freedom to Share

Projects may be redistributed under the terms of the FAIL license.

---

## Transparency

Projects should include clear documentation whenever practical.

# Governance

FAIL is maintained by the **Free AI License Foundation**.

The Foundation is responsible for:

- Maintaining the license
- Publishing new versions
- Reviewing proposed changes
- Coordinating community discussions
- Maintaining specifications
- Managing the registry

Major changes should follow a documented public review process.

---

# Versioning

FAIL follows semantic versioning principles where appropriate.

- Major versions introduce significant changes.
- Minor versions add clarifications or new optional features.
- Patch versions correct errors or improve documentation without changing the intended meaning.

Projects should clearly indicate the FAIL version they use.

---

# Compatibility

FAIL is designed to be easy to adopt.

Before combining FAIL-licensed material with content under other licenses, developers should review the terms of each applicable license to ensure compatibility.

The Foundation may publish compatibility guidance as the ecosystem evolves.

---

# Security

Projects are encouraged to include:

- SECURITY.md
- Responsible disclosure procedures
- Security contact information
- AI safety documentation
- Known limitations

Security documentation improves trust and operational resilience.

---

# Transparency

Projects should document:

- Model purpose
- Intended use
- Limitations
- Training methodology
- Evaluation process
- Safety considerations

These recommendations support responsible deployment but are not automatically legal requirements unless stated in the LICENSE.

---

# Commercial Use

FAIL permits commercial use under the terms defined in the LICENSE.

Commercial organizations may develop products and services using FAIL-licensed projects while complying with applicable license conditions.
