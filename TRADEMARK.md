# Purpose

This Trademark Policy explains how the names, logos and visual identity of the **Free AI License (FAIL)** may be used.

The goal of this policy is to protect the integrity, reputation and identity of the FAIL ecosystem while allowing fair and accurate references.

This policy is separate from the FAIL software license.

---

# Covered Trademarks

The following names, logos and identifiers are trademarks or intended trademarks of the **Free AI License Foundation**.

- Free AI License
- FAIL
- FAIL Foundation
- Free AI License Foundation
- Official FAIL Logo
- Official FAIL Badge System
- Official FAIL Certification Marks

Additional names, logos or visual assets published by the Foundation may also be protected.

---

# Permitted Uses

You may use the FAIL name to truthfully describe your project.

Examples include:

- Licensed under FAIL
- Compatible with FAIL
- Built using FAIL
- FAIL-licensed project

You may also reference FAIL in:

- Research papers
- Blog posts
- Documentation
- Tutorials
- Educational material
- Conference presentations
- News articles

provided the reference is truthful and does not imply endorsement.

---

# Logo Usage

The official FAIL logo may be used only when:

- Referring to the FAIL license.
- Linking to the official FAIL repository or website.
- Identifying a project that is officially licensed under FAIL.
- Displaying official Foundation materials.

The logo must not be modified.

---

# You Must Not

You may not:

- Modify the official logo.
- Change the logo colors.
- Stretch or distort the logo.
- Animate the logo in a misleading manner.
- Remove identifying elements.
- Create confusingly similar logos.
- Claim that your project is officially maintained by the Foundation unless it is.

---

# Project Names

Projects may state:

> Licensed under FAIL

or

> Uses the Free AI License

However, project names should not imply they are official Foundation projects unless authorized.

Examples:

✅ MyAI (Licensed under FAIL)

✅ Vision Agent using FAIL

❌ Official FAIL Vision

❌ FAIL Studio

❌ FAIL Enterprise

without written authorization.

---

# Certification Marks

Official certification marks such as:

- FAIL Verified
- FAIL Core
- FAIL Plus
- FAIL Enterprise

may only be displayed by projects that have successfully completed the applicable certification process.

Unauthorized use of certification marks is prohibited.

---

# Domains

You should not register domain names that could reasonably confuse users into believing they represent the Foundation.

Examples of domains that generally require authorization include:

- failfoundation.org
- officialfail.org
- faillicense.org

---

# Social Media

Community accounts are welcome.

However, accounts must not pretend to be official Foundation accounts.

Examples:

✅ @FailCommunity

✅ @FailDevelopers

❌ @OfficialFAIL

❌ @FAILFoundation

unless authorized.

---

# Fair Use

The Foundation recognizes fair use of its trademarks for purposes such as:

- Commentary
- Criticism
- Education
- News reporting
- Academic research

provided there is no suggestion of official sponsorship or endorsement.

---

# Commercial Use

Commercial companies may accurately state that their products are licensed under FAIL or compatible with FAIL.

They may not imply endorsement, partnership or certification by the Foundation unless such a relationship exists.

---

# Enforcement

The Foundation may request correction or removal of trademark uses that:

- Cause confusion.
- Misrepresent affiliation.
- Misuse official branding.
- Damage the reputation of the FAIL ecosystem.

Whenever practical, the Foundation will seek to resolve issues through communication before taking further action.
