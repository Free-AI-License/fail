# General

## What is FAIL?

The **Free AI License (FAIL)** is an open license designed for Artificial Intelligence projects. It allows individuals and organizations to use, study, modify, distribute and build upon AI technologies while promoting openness, transparency and collaboration.

---

## What does FAIL stand for?

**FAIL** stands for **Free AI License**.

---

## Who maintains FAIL?

FAIL is maintained by the **Free AI License Foundation** together with its open community of contributors.

---

## Is FAIL open?

Yes.

FAIL is an open license intended for public use.

---

## Is FAIL free?

Yes.

There are no licensing fees or royalties required to use FAIL.

---

# Permissions

## Can I use FAIL for commercial projects?

Yes.

Commercial use is permitted under the terms of the license.

---

## Can I build a startup using FAIL?

Yes.

Individuals, startups, universities, nonprofits and enterprises may build products and services using FAIL-licensed projects.

---

## Can I sell products built with FAIL?

Yes.

FAIL allows commercial distribution and commercial services.

---

## Can I build SaaS or cloud services?

Yes.

Cloud-hosted AI services and APIs are allowed.

---

## Can I modify FAIL-licensed software or models?

Yes.

You may study, modify and improve FAIL-licensed projects.

---

## Can I redistribute my modified version?

Yes.

Redistribution is permitted under the terms of the license.

---

## Can I train new AI models using FAIL projects?

Yes.

Training, fine-tuning and research are allowed unless restricted by third-party assets included in a project.

---

# Obligations

## Do I have to keep attribution?

Yes.

Original copyright notices and license information must be preserved.

---

## Do I have to publish my modifications?

No.

Publishing improvements is **encouraged**, but it is **not a legal requirement** of the FAIL license.

---

## Do I have to open-source my entire project?

No.

FAIL does not require every project using FAIL-licensed components to become open source unless the license text explicitly states otherwise.

---

## Can I combine FAIL with other licenses?

It depends.

Compatibility varies by license. Review both licenses before combining code or models.

---

# Legal

## Does FAIL provide a patent grant?

See the official LICENSE document for the complete legal terms regarding patents.

---

## Does FAIL include a warranty?

No.

Projects are generally provided **"AS IS"**, without warranties, as described in the LICENSE.

---

## Is FAIL legally reviewed?

The official text is maintained by the Free AI License Foundation. Organizations adopting FAIL are encouraged to obtain independent legal advice when needed.

---

## Is FAIL approved by the Open Source Initiative (OSI)?

Not necessarily.

Unless officially approved by the OSI, FAIL should not be described as an OSI-approved license.
