## 1. Purpose

The Free Artificial Intelligence License ("FAIL" or "this License") is an
open licensing framework designed exclusively for Artificial Intelligence
Assets. Its purpose is to encourage innovation, research, education,
interoperability, and commercial adoption of Artificial Intelligence while
preserving openness and legal certainty for both creators and recipients.

## 2. Scope

This License applies exclusively to the following categories of works
("Artificial Intelligence Assets" or "Assets"):

- **Artificial Intelligence Models** — foundation models, language models,
  multimodal models, diffusion models, speech models, vision models, and
  other machine learning models.
- **Model Weights** — trained parameters, checkpoints, quantized weights,
  adapters, LoRA files, and derivative weight files.
- **Datasets** — training datasets, fine-tuning datasets, evaluation
  datasets, synthetic datasets, benchmarks, embeddings, and associated
  metadata.
- **Artificial Intelligence APIs** — inference APIs, training APIs,
  embedding APIs, agent APIs, multimodal APIs, SDKs, and AI service
  interfaces.

This License does **not** apply to general-purpose software, operating
systems, libraries, hardware, firmware, or databases unrelated to AI,
unless such works are expressly and separately distributed under FAIL as
Artificial Intelligence Assets.

Any software accompanying an Artificial Intelligence Asset (for example,
training scripts, inference servers, or tooling) may be distributed under
a separate software license chosen by its copyright holder. FAIL is
designed to coexist with software licenses such as MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD,
GPL, LGPL, and MPL: software components may remain under their original
license while the Artificial Intelligence Assets themselves are governed
by FAIL.

Future versions of FAIL may extend coverage to additional categories of
AI technologies while preserving backward compatibility whenever
reasonably possible.

## 3. Definitions

**"Artificial Intelligence Model"** means a machine learning model,
neural network, language model, multimodal model, diffusion model, speech
model, vision model, or similar technology.

**"Model Weights"** means trained parameters, checkpoints, adapters, LoRA
files, quantized models, and derivative weight files.

**"Dataset"** means any collection of information intended for training,
evaluation, benchmarking, or fine-tuning of AI systems.

**"API"** means any application programming interface providing access to
Artificial Intelligence functionality.

**"Work"** means any Artificial Intelligence Asset distributed under this
License.

**"Derivative AI Asset"** means any Work created by modifying,
fine-tuning, quantizing, merging, distilling, or otherwise adapting a
Work, including but not limited to fine-tuned models, quantized weights,
adapters (e.g., LoRA), model merges, and derivative checkpoints.

**"Contributor"** means any person or organization submitting
modifications or improvements to a Work.

**"Recipient"** means any individual or organization receiving a Work
under this License.

**"License Steward"** means the entity responsible for maintaining,
publishing, interpreting, and evolving this License, as defined in
Section 16.
