# FAIL Manifesto
## Free AI License Manifesto

**Version 1.0**

---

# We Believe

Artificial Intelligence is one of humanity's greatest inventions.

It has the potential to accelerate scientific discovery, improve education, advance medicine, empower creators, strengthen businesses, and solve problems once thought impossible.

Its benefits should not belong only to a handful of organizations.

They should belong to everyone.

---

# Our Vision

We envision a future where every student, developer, researcher, entrepreneur, teacher, artist, scientist, startup, nonprofit, company, and government can access Artificial Intelligence without unnecessary barriers.

Knowledge should never be locked behind exclusive access.

Innovation should never depend solely on financial resources.

Opportunity should be available to all.

---

# Our Principles

## Freedom

Everyone should be free to use Artificial Intelligence.

Everyone should be free to learn from it.

Everyone should be free to improve it.

Everyone should be free to build with it.

Everyone should be free to share it.

---

## Openness

Open technologies create stronger ecosystems.

Transparency builds trust.

Collaboration accelerates innovation.

Knowledge grows when it is shared.

---

## Responsibility

Freedom carries responsibility.

Artificial Intelligence should be developed and used in ways that respect people, human rights, applicable laws, and the broader public interest.

Open access is not an invitation to misuse technology.

---

## Innovation

Innovation does not belong to one company, one country, or one generation.

History has shown that open ecosystems often become the foundation for the next wave of technological progress.

Artificial Intelligence should continue that tradition.

---

## Community

The future of AI should be shaped by a global community.

Researchers.

Developers.

Educators.

Students.

Companies.

Governments.

Nonprofit organizations.

Independent creators.

Everyone has a place.

---

# Why FAIL Exists

The Free AI License (FAIL) exists to provide an open legal framework specifically designed for Artificial Intelligence.

It allows people to:

- Use AI.
- Study AI.
- Modify AI.
- Share AI.
- Build products with AI.
- Create businesses using AI.
- Advance research through AI.

while preserving attribution and encouraging collaboration.

---

# What We Protect

We protect the freedom to innovate.

We protect access to knowledge.

We protect collaboration.

We protect scientific progress.

We protect future generations' ability to learn and build.

---

# What We Reject

We reject unnecessary barriers to learning.

We reject artificial restrictions that prevent innovation.

We reject the idea that only a small group should benefit from Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Our Commitment

The Free AI License will continue evolving through open discussion, public review, and community participation.

Every new version should make AI more accessible, more understandable, and more useful for humanity.

---

# Our Mission

To ensure that Artificial Intelligence remains open, accessible, and available for everyone.

Forever.

---

# Our Motto

> **Artificial Intelligence belongs to everyone.**

> **Freedom to learn. Freedom to build. Freedom to innovate.**

---

Whether you are building your first AI project or leading a global organization, you are invited to participate.

Use FAIL.

Improve FAIL.

Share FAIL.

Help build an open future for Artificial Intelligence.

Together, we can ensure that AI remains a technology that empowers humanity.
