# Abstract

The FAIL (Free AI License) Specification defines the technical scope, terminology, and implementation guidelines for applying the Free AI License to Artificial Intelligence technologies.

Unlike traditional software licenses, FAIL is specifically designed for AI ecosystems, including machine learning models, datasets, agents, inference systems, and future intelligent technologies.

This specification is intended to provide consistency across projects adopting FAIL.

---

# Goals

FAIL aims to:

- Keep Artificial Intelligence open.
- Encourage innovation.
- Protect attribution.
- Enable commercial adoption.
- Promote interoperability.
- Reduce legal uncertainty.
- Support education and research.
- Foster global collaboration.

# Definitions

## Model

A machine learning model consisting of architecture, weights, configuration and associated metadata.

---

## Dataset

A structured collection of information used for training, evaluation or fine-tuning AI systems.

---

## Weights

The numerical parameters produced during training that determine the model's behavior.

---

## Checkpoint

A saved version of a trained model.

---

## Derivative Work

Any modified version or new work created from a FAIL licensed asset.

---

## Contributor

Any individual or organization submitting improvements or modifications.

---

## Distribution

Making the software or model available to another party.

---

## Commercial Use

Any activity intended to generate revenue directly or indirectly.

# Versioning

FAIL follows semantic versioning.

Major

Breaking legal or structural changes.

Minor

New sections or clarifications.

Patch

Editorial corrections.

Example

FAIL 1.0.0

FAIL 1.1.0

FAIL 2.0.0

---

# Compliance

A project is considered FAIL compliant when:

The LICENSE file is included.

Attribution is preserved.

The license text remains unmodified.

Copyright notices remain intact.

The project complies with applicable laws.
